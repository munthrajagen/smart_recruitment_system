How It Works

Simple, structured workflow for both employers and job seekers.

For Recruiters

  1. Register Employer Account: Sign up with your company email credentials.
  2. Post Vacancies: Publish job requirements, location, salary, and experience criteria.
  3. Review & Shortlist: Inspect candidate applications, view resumes, and update status.

For Candidates

  1. Create Profile: Register as candidate and upload your latest resume.
  2. Search & Filter Jobs: Browse available job openings matching your skillset.
  3. One-Click Apply & Track: Submit your resume and monitor application progress in real-time.
